An unofficial concept, not affiliated with or endorsed by Exolab S.r.l.

## What it is

A redesign proposal for exolab.it, the site of the Italian IT consultancy I work for. It's one page that walks through the services, how a project runs, the training courses, a strip of clients and the contact forms. A WebGL hero opens it, and 3D scenes move as you scroll.

## How it's built

React and strict TypeScript on Vite, styled with Tailwind CSS. The 3D scenes are three.js, kept in plain modules apart from the page sections, and Vitest with Testing Library covers both.

## Why there's nothing to open yet

To look real, the concept uses Exolab's own copy and its clients' names and logos, and some of those clients ask for permission before their logos are shown. I'll publish the repository once that's cleared.
